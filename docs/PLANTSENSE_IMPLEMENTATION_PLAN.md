Sequential implementation schedule for the PlantSense Monitoring System mobile prototype. Every phase
begins after the final milestone of the previous phase. The plan includes Flutter development for Android and
iOS, secure AI image analysis, user accounts, plant and care management, analytics, testing, role
assignments, and continuous completion of the graduation thesis. Physical IoT cameras and environmental
sensors remain future work.
